Note Walker privacy policy

Last updated November 16, 2026

Note Walker is a rhythm-reading game for music class. It is built to collect nothing about the people who play it.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases. It does not use the internet while you play.

What stays on your device

Your stars, settings, high scores and the nicknames typed into the high score list are saved only on the device you play on. They are never sent anywhere. Deleting the app (or clearing this website's data in your browser) erases them.

Children

Because the game collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children.

Changes

If this ever changes, this page will be updated first, with a new date at the top.
