Note Walker help

A rhythm-reading game for music class, by Mansfield Games.

How to play

Notes scroll toward the ring. Tap once, anywhere, as each note reaches it. Stay still for rests, and for a tie tap only the first note. Extra taps count against you.

Learn has nine short levels, from quarter notes to sixteenths, with up to three stars on each. Marathon is one long run that keeps getting harder, with three lives and a high score list.

Common questions

There's no sound.
Turn the volume up with the side buttons. If you are playing on the website, also check that the browser tab isn't muted.
My stars or high scores are gone.
They are saved only on the device and browser you played on. Deleting the app, or clearing the website's data, erases them.
My taps seem a little late.
Bluetooth headphones and speakers add a delay. Wired headphones or the device's own speaker work best.
